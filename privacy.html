Privacy Policy

How Dohar Car Repair collects, uses, and protects your information

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Dohar Car Repair (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the website doharcarrepairqa.com and provides automotive repair and related services in Doha, Qatar. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Contact form submissions

When you use our contact form or reach us by email, phone, or WhatsApp, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Service requested and message details (for example vehicle make, model, or repair description)

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry, provide quotes, schedule services, and follow up on your request.

General site analytics

Like most websites, we may collect general, non-identifying technical information about visits to our site. This can include browser type, device type, approximate location (country/city level), pages viewed, and referring URLs. This helps us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve content and performance. We do not use analytics data to sell your personal information.

2. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They may be used to:

  • Remember basic preferences and improve site functionality
  • Measure traffic and usage patterns (analytics)
  • Support advertising features described below

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how certain parts of the site work.

3. Google AdSense and Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google and its partners use cookies (and similar technologies) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and/or other websites on the Internet. This helps Google and partners show more relevant ads and measure ad performance.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s previous visits to this or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings at https://www.google.com/settings/ads. You can also learn more about how Google uses data when you use our partners’ sites or apps at Google’s Partner Sites Policy.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Respond to contact requests and provide automotive service quotes
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website
  • Display advertising through Google AdSense
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable

We do not sell your personal contact details to third parties for their marketing.

5. Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers who help us run the website or communicate with you (for example email or hosting providers), only as needed to perform those services. Advertising partners such as Google may receive cookie and usage data as described in the AdSense section above. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

6. Data Retention and Security

We keep contact inquiries for as long as needed to handle your request and for legitimate business records. We take reasonable steps to protect information we hold, but no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

7. Your Choices

  • You may request updates or deletion of contact information you previously sent us by emailing us.
  • You may opt out of personalized ads via Google Ads Settings.
  • You may disable cookies in your browser.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are directed to vehicle owners and the general public seeking automotive services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can delete it.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the website after changes means you accept the updated policy.
