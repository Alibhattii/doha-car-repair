Our 24/7 Roadside Assistance Services in Qatar

Car Towing Service

If your vehicle cannot be repaired on the roadside, we provide safe flatbed towing to our workshop or any destination in Qatar. We handle all vehicle types — sedans, SUVs, luxury cars, and 4x4s — with proper equipment that protects your vehicle's drivetrain and bodywork during transport.

Battery Jumpstart

Dead battery is the number one roadside call-out in Qatar. Heat kills car batteries faster than anywhere else — a battery that works fine in winter can fail suddenly in summer. Our team carries professional jump-start equipment for all vehicle types including those with Start-Stop systems that require smart chargers.

Flat Tyre Change

We change flat tyres roadside and fit your spare safely. If you don't have a spare, we can tow you to the nearest tyre shop or supply a replacement tyre on-site. Never change a tyre yourself on Qatar's fast highways — always call for professional roadside assistance.

Emergency Fuel Delivery

Run out of fuel on a Qatar highway? We deliver emergency fuel to your location. We supply petrol (91 or 95 octane) and diesel anywhere in Qatar, getting you moving in under 30 minutes in most Doha locations.

Car Lockout Service

Locked your keys inside the car? We use professional non-destructive entry tools to open your vehicle without damaging locks or windows. Available 24/7 across all Qatar including Doha, Lusail, Al Wakra, and Al Rayyan.

Engine Overheating Assistance

An overheated engine on Qatar's summer roads is a common emergency. Our technician arrives with coolant and diagnostic tools to assess whether a simple coolant top-up is sufficient or if the vehicle needs towing for deeper repairs.

Minor Roadside Repairs

Many breakdowns can be fixed on the spot — loose battery terminals, snapped serpentine belt, empty washer fluid causing sensor warnings, disconnected hoses. Our technicians carry common spare parts and tools for quick roadside fixes.

What to Do When Your Car Breaks Down in Qatar

  1. Pull Over Safely

    Move to the hard shoulder or nearest service road. Switch on hazard lights immediately and stay visible. On Qatar highways, do not stand behind the vehicle — move to the verge or barrier side.

  2. Turn On Hazard Lights

    Hazard lights are your primary visibility tool. Turn them on the moment you sense a problem, not after stopping — this gives following traffic maximum warning time.

  3. Place Warning Triangles

    Qatar traffic law requires placing warning triangles behind your vehicle. Place one 50 metres back and another 100 metres back on motorways.

  4. Call Dohar Car Repair

    Call or WhatsApp 0097472223959. Tell us your exact location (use Google Maps pin), the type of problem, and your vehicle make and model. We dispatch immediately.

  5. Stay in the Car if Safe

    On high-speed roads, remain in your vehicle with your seatbelt on if possible. Only exit if there is a fire or immediate safety threat.

  6. Wait for Our Team

    Our response time in Doha city is typically 20–40 minutes. On remote Qatar highways, allow 45–60 minutes. We keep you updated via WhatsApp.
