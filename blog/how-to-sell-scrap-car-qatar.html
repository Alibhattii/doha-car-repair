Scrap Sale vs Accident Sale: Know the Difference

Before you prepare papers, confirm which intent matches your situation:

  • Scrap / unwanted / end-of-life: Old, non-running, junk, or no longer worth keeping. Follow this guide, then contact our scrap car buying service.
  • Accident / collision / total loss: Crash damage, airbags deployed, or insurance settlement cases. Use the accident car buyer page so evaluation focuses on damage and claim status.

Choosing the right path helps the buyer ask the right questions and keeps your offer closer to real market value.

Step-by-Step: Process to Sell a Scrap Car in Qatar

  1. Decide the car is for scrap or disposal

    Confirm you will not repair it further. Note whether it runs, where it is parked, and any missing major parts.

  2. Gather ownership documents

    Locate the Mulkiya (vehicle registration card), your QID or passport, and any bank clearance if the car is financed. Missing papers slow every sale.

  3. Take clear photos

    Capture all sides, the interior, odometer if accessible, and any obvious damage. Send these when you request an evaluation.

  4. Request a free evaluation

    Contact a reputable buyer such as Dohar Car Repair for a free on-site or photo evaluation based on current market rates — not a random street estimate.

  5. Review the offer and ask questions

    Understand what the price covers. A transparent buyer explains parts value and scrap value without last-minute deductions.

  6. Complete paperwork and transfer

    Professional buyers handle transfer, deregistration, and documentation. Do not hand over the car without clear ownership transfer arrangements.

  7. Arrange pickup and payment

    Free pickup across Doha and surrounding areas is available with Dohar Car Repair. Payment is completed as agreed once documents are in order.

Documents First, Pickup Second

Have Mulkiya and ID ready before the buyer arrives. Prepared sellers finish faster — especially when free pickup is already scheduled the same day.

Documents You Typically Need

As outlined in our site FAQ for scrap sales in Qatar, you typically need:

  • Vehicle registration card (Mulkiya)
  • Qatari ID (QID) or passport of the owner
  • Proof of ownership when additional verification is required
  • Bank / finance clearance if the vehicle is still under loan

Important Things to Know Before You Sell

Valuation Is Market-Based

Scrap and parts prices change. Expect a free evaluation rather than a published fixed rate. Make, model, year, completeness, and whether the car runs all influence the offer.

Paperwork Matters More Than Paint

A complete Mulkiya and clear ownership usually matter more than cosmetic condition for scrap sales. Finance holds must be cleared before transfer.

Free Pickup Does Not Mean Skip Documents

Pickup removes the vehicle from your location; paperwork still transfers legal responsibility. Use a buyer who handles transfer and deregistration for you.

Personal Items and Plates

Remove personal belongings, garage remotes, and any items you want to keep before collection day. Ask the buyer what happens with number plates under their process.

Do Not Strip Valuable Parts Blindly

Removing random parts without knowing market demand can reduce the buyer’s offer more than the cash you get selling pieces separately. Ask during evaluation if you are unsure.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Accepting cash with no transfer plan: Always complete proper ownership transfer
  • Mixing scrap intent with accident claims: Use the correct buyer path for insurance cases
  • Waiting months after the car dies: Storage fees and missing parts can reduce value
  • Ignoring finance clearance: Bank NOC issues block paperwork at the last step
  • Sending unclear photos: Dark or incomplete pictures delay evaluation

Yes. Non-running cars are commonly sold as scrap. Tell the buyer access details so free pickup can be planned.
