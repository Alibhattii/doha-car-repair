What Affects Your Scrap Car Valuation

Every offer is based on a free evaluation. Typical factors include:

  • Make, model, and year: High-demand brands often retain stronger parts value
  • Running vs non-running: Driveable cars can be easier to inspect and move
  • Major components present: Engine, gearbox, and intact body panels still matter for scrap buyers
  • Completeness: Missing wheels, catalytic converters, or electronics can lower value
  • Location and access: Easy pickup helps keep the process quick
