What Counts as an Accident Car in Qatar?

At Dohar Car Repair, we buy vehicles in all accident-related conditions:

  • Collision Damage: Front, rear, or side impact — from minor to severe
  • Total Loss (Write-Off): Insurance-declared total loss vehicles
  • Rollover Damage: Cars that have rolled or flipped
  • Airbag Deployment: Vehicles where all airbags have deployed
  • Structural Damage: Bent chassis or frame damage
  • Flood-Damaged Cars: Vehicles water-damaged in floods or storms
  • Fire-Damaged Cars: Vehicles with engine or interior fire damage
  • Cars with Pending Insurance Claims: We help navigate the process

More Value Than You Think

Many Qatar drivers underestimate the value of their accident car. A heavily damaged vehicle still contains valuable components — engine parts, gearbox, electronics, suspension components, and recyclable metals. A professional buyer like us evaluates all of this to offer you a true market price.

What Affects the Value of Your Accident Car?

Severity of Damage

The extent of structural, mechanical, and cosmetic damage directly impacts value. A car with a damaged front bumper but an intact engine and chassis is worth significantly more than one with a bent frame and seized engine.

Working Major Components

If the engine, gearbox, AC compressor, alternator, or other major mechanical parts still function, your accident car commands a premium price. We assess these individually before making an offer.

Vehicle Make, Model, and Year

High-demand models (Toyota Land Cruiser, Prado, Hilux, Nissan Patrol, Lexus) retain high part values even when badly damaged, often resulting in excellent offers from specialist buyers in Qatar.

Mileage

Lower-mileage accident cars are worth more because their mechanical components have less wear and higher resale value in the used parts market.

Insurance Status

If your insurance company has paid out and transferred ownership, the process is cleaner and faster. If the claim is still pending, we can work with you through that process.

Documents Needed to Sell an Accident Car in Qatar
